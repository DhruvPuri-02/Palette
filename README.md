# 🎨 Art Market Hub

Art Market Hub is a modern online marketplace designed to connect artists with buyers through a social-media-inspired platform. Artists can showcase their original artwork, sell pieces directly, or host auctions, while buyers can discover unique creations, interact with artists, and build their collections.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Traditional online marketplaces often lack a dedicated space where artists can build a community around their work. Art Market Hub aims to bridge that gap by combining artwork discovery, social engagement, direct sales, and auctions into a single platform.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🎨 For Artists
- Create and manage an artist profile
- Upload and showcase artwork
- Add artwork details such as title, description, category, and price
- Choose between:
  - Direct Sale
  - Auction Listing
- Manage artwork listings

### 🛍️ For Buyers
- Browse artwork from different artists
- Search and filter artwork
- View detailed artwork pages
- Purchase artwork directly
- Participate in artwork auctions
- View auction status and highest bids

### 🌐 Community
- Like artwork
- Comment on artwork
- Discover new artists
- Social-style artwork feed

---

## 🚀 MVP Goals

The initial version focuses on validating the core marketplace experience by providing:

- Artist registration
- Artwork uploads
- Artwork feed
- Direct artwork purchases
- Auction creation
- Auction bidding
- User authentication
- Basic profile management
